About Me

Design is solving problems with empathy and creativity.

I am a Graphic and UX/UI Designer with 6+ years of experience in graphic design, branding, marketing collateral, social media management, and user-centered digital experiences. I specialize in creating engaging visual content, designing intuitive interfaces, developing brand identities, and supporting marketing campaigns across digital platforms.

I collaborate with clients, developers, and marketing teams to transform ideas into effective design solutions that align with business goals. My skills include visual storytelling, layout design, wireframing, prototyping, and maintaining brand consistency across multiple touchpoints.

Proficient in Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, Figma, CorelDRAW, CapCut, and Canva Pro, I also leverage AI tools like Claude and Google Gemini for creative ideation, content development, research, and improving design workflows. I am passionate about combining creativity, technology, and user-focused thinking to deliver meaningful digital and visual experiences.
